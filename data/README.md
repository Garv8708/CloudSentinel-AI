Place your permitted training CSV here and name it `network_traffic.csv`.

Suggested starting dataset: UCI RT-IoT2022:
https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/942/rt-iot2022

The training script detects common target names. Inspect the dataset first and, if necessary, set `TARGET_COLUMN` in `src/config.py`.

Datasets are intentionally not bundled in this ZIP. They may be large and have their own terms of use. Do not upload private or confidential network data.
